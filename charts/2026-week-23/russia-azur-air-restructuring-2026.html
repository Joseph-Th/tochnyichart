Azur Air — Restructuring Cuts Staff by 40–50%

Russia's charter airline enters restructuring; workforce to shrink from 1,500 to an estimated 750–900 employees

▼ 40–50%
staff reduction
Azur Air is Russia's largest charter carrier. Restructuring reflects sustained pressure from closed international routes, high fuel costs, and weakening leisure travel demand. Post-restructuring headcount: 750–900 workers.
