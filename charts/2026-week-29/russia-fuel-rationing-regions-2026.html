Rationing Has Reached Everyday Life

Governors have been instructed not to ask Moscow for fuel — they must divide what arrives and absorb the public anger. Here is what that looks like across nine regions this week

76 minutes
Zabaykalsky Krai
Online booking introduced to ration a week of fuel. Every slot was claimed in 76 minutes.
20% fewer buses
Yekaterinburg
One in five buses pulled from public routes because there is not enough fuel to run them.
Irregular pickup
Omsk
Garbage collection declared "irregular" — in the city that houses Russia's largest refinery.
Paper coupons
Krasnoyarsk Krai
A large regional station chain abandoned open sales and moved customers onto fuel coupons.
Overnight queues
Ufa, Bashkortostan
Queues stretching kilometres through the night across the city after strikes on local refineries.
Rural blackout
Tver Oblast
Large parts of the region had no station selling fuel at all following strikes on depots.
Southern third dry
Republic of Yakutia
The head of the republic travelled to Moscow to plead for fuel. Rationing and long queues in place.
Quota too small
Krasnodar Krai
The governor appealed publicly despite the ban, saying the farm fuel quota cannot bring in the harvest.
Booking system next
Altai Krai
Scheduled to adopt the same slot-booking scheme that collapsed in Zabaykalsky Krai.
Source: Russian regional administrations and local reporting, compiled by Tochnyi — July 2026
