Turning the Lights Off on Fuel Pricing

The share of Russian diesel that must be sold on the open SPIMEX exchange was cut from 16% to 10% — pushing 9 of every 10 litres into opaque bilateral deals

Staff cuts
SPIMEX began reducing headcount as volumes traded on it shrink
Buyers restricted
Only end users and retailers may now purchase fuel on the exchange
Price signal lost
The exchange is Russia's only public benchmark for wholesale fuel
