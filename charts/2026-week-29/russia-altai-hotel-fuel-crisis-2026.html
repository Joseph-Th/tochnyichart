Date: 2026-07-19

Hotels Are Now Sourcing Their Guests' Fuel

In the Republic of Altai, the same collapse seen in Crimean hotels in May and June is repeating: guests cannot be sure they can drive home, so hotels are buying fuel for them to stop the cancellations

1
Visitors stop coming by car
With no guarantee of fuel for the return journey, drivers from outside the region cancel rather than risk being stranded.
2
Discounts for outsiders
Hotels offer price cuts specifically to guests arriving from other regions, plus free extra nights to make the trip worth the risk.
3
Locals carousel the pumps
Residents queue repeatedly at filling stations to accumulate fuel, then resell it to visiting guests so they can drive home.
4
Small hotels near bankruptcy
Cancellations are pushing family-owned guesthouses — which have no cash buffer for a lost season — to the edge of collapse.
Source: Russian regional tourism reporting, Republic of Altai and Crimea, compiled by Tochnyi — July 2026
