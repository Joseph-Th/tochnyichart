The Scale of the App the EU Just Sanctioned

Brussels sanctioned VK over its work on the Russian state messenger MAX — an app with 120 million registered users that is pre-installed on every phone sold in Russia. Apple and Google then pulled every VK app

All VK apps pulled
The App Store and Google Play removed VK's entire catalogue after the listing
Pre-installed by law
MAX has shipped on every smartphone sold in Russia since September 2025
State surveillance
The EU cited VK's cooperation with the Russian government in building MAX
