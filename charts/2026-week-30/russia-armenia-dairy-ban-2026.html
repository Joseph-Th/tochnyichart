Four Fifths of a Country's Dairy Trade, Cut Off

From 27 July Russia bans dairy imports from every Armenian producer. Around 80% of Armenia's dairy exports went to Russia, worth an estimated 16 million dollars a year

16 million
US dollars of annual losses to Armenia
Cheese takes the hit
Cheese accounts for around 85% of Armenian dairy exports, so cheesemakers bear most of the loss
Every producer, at once
The restriction applies to all Armenian dairy enterprises, not to named violators
Part of a pattern
Follows bans on Armenian flowers, mineral water, vegetables and fruit imposed since late May
Source: Rosselkhoznadzor import restriction notice and Armenian dairy export statistics, compiled by Tochnyi — July 2026
