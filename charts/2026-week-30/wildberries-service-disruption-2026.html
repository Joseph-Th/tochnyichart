Where Russia's Largest Marketplace Stopped Working

The strikes on Wildberries warehouses did not just destroy buildings. Across four regions the service itself has degraded or failed outright, and the businesses that depend on it have no alternative supplier

Crimea
Service withdrawn entirely
The peninsula lost all coverage in the application. Customers cannot place an order at all.
Belarus
7 day delivery waits
Customers are waiting a full week for orders that previously arrived in days.
Southern Russia
Pick-up points receiving nothing
Served by the destroyed Krasnodar and Stavropol warehouses. These are private businesses with no incoming goods and therefore no revenue — they have asked the government for financial support until deliveries resume.
North-Western Russia
Sellers blocked from their own stock
Sellers trying to ship goods out of warehouses were refused, then told they could only do so at their own cost and only to a pick-up point — all of which were already overloaded with returns and fire sales.
Source: Wildberries customer and seller reports across affected regions, compiled by Tochnyi — July 2026
