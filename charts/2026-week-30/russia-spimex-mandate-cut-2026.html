Closing Down the Open Fuel Market

In May refineries had to sell 15% of their output on the open exchange. The requirement is now 10%, and the government plans to cut it to just 2% — leaving independent buyers with almost nowhere to bid

13,000 tons a day lost
Fall in mandated daily exchange supply between the May rule and the planned 2% rule
Allocation, not trade
With almost nothing on the exchange, fuel is distributed administratively rather than bought
Independents squeezed hardest
Independent stations and regional buyers rely on the exchange because they have no refinery of their own
Source: Russian government exchange-sale requirements, compiled by Tochnyi — 2026. Tonnages scaled from exchange volumes at the 10% rule
