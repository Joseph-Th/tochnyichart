Two Hundred Thousand More Cards Than a Year Ago

Russian banks issued 1.22 million new credit cards in June 2026 — around 203,000 more than in June 2025, though issuance has already turned down from the May peak

203,000 more cards
Increase against June 2025, a rise of 19.9% year on year
54,000 fewer than May
Issuance fell 4.2% month on month, the first sign of a turn
Borrowing to cope
Card growth continues despite a key rate of 14%, pointing to households bridging costs rather than spending freely
