Date: 2026-07-26

The Shortage Moved East, It Did Not End

Moscow and Saint Petersburg improved this week. Across the south and beyond the Urals, hospitals, fire brigades and whole cities still cannot reliably buy fuel

Critical — essential services affected
Vladimir region
5 hospitals found no seller willing to bid on their fuel tenders, including the region's main emergency unit.
Krasnoyarsk
Local fuel companies failed to supply the fire service. A criminal case has been opened against them.
Tyumen
A special station schedule was introduced just to refuel emergency vehicles. Supply worsened again after the latest strike on the local refinery.
Severe — public order and closures
Kursk region
Over a third of all filling stations have closed down.
Mari El capital
Volunteers stand at filling stations all day to keep order in the queues.
Novosibirsk
Russia's third largest city remains gripped by a crippling shortage.
Kirov region
Crippling fuel shortages continue with no sign of easing.
Pskov region
No improvement recorded over the week.
Southern Russia
Large queues persist wherever fuel depots continued to be struck.
Improved
Moscow and Saint Petersburg
Noticeable improvement in availability over the week.
Irkutsk
The only improvement east of the Urals — arranged for Putin's visit, with prices ordered down to 68 rubles a litre.
Everywhere else in the east
No measurable improvement recorded.
Source: Regional fuel availability reports across the Russian Federation, compiled by Tochnyi — July 2026
