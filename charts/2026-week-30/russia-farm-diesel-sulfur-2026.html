Nine Hundred Times the Sulfur Limit

The Ministry of Energy asked whether Russian farmers would accept diesel containing 0.3% to 0.9% sulfur, or heating oil outright. Modern engines require no more than 0.001% — this fuel would destroy them

Engines written off

Harvest quotas
The Ministry of Agriculture is proposing regional diesel quotas, conceding there is not enough for the harvest
The only surplus left
High-sulfur fuel appears to be the sole product Russian buyers can currently be offered in volume
