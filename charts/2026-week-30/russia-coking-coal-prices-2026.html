Coal Prices Rose Because Energy Ran Short

Russian coking coal is up sharply everywhere it is sold, with eastern port export prices up 57.6% year on year. The driver is the general shortage of fuel and energy, not any recovery in steel demand

Steel demand fell 7.5%

India and China buying

Expected to stall
Price growth is expected to halt by the third quarter as blast furnaces go in for repairs
